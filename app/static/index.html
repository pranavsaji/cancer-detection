        Method

        Each image is sent once to POST /v1/decisions with the gpt-6-luna model and three questions: a yes/no on malignancy, a pick-one diagnosis, and a check that the picture shows a skin lesion. The model sees only the image, with no patient history.

        Test images come from HAM10000 in the public ISIC Archive: one image per lesion, drawn at random with a fixed seed, half benign and half malignant. That balance is deliberate and far from a real clinic, where most lesions are benign, so precision here overstates what you would see in practice.

        The 50% cut-off was fixed before the run. Moving the slider shows the trade-off; it does not make any other cut-off a validated result. Ranges are 95% Wilson intervals.
